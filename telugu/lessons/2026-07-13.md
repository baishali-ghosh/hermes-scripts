# Telugu Lesson — Day 36 (2026-07-13)

**Week 8 · Lesson 1/5 · 🏙️ Places & Directions**

- **Telugu:** ఇది ఎక్కడ ఉంది?
- **Speak:** /Idi ekkada undi?/
- **Meaning:** Where is this?
- **Example:** రైల్వే స్టేషన్ ఎక్కడ ఉంది? — Where is the railway station?
- **Tip:** 'ఎక్కడ' = where. One of the most used question words
