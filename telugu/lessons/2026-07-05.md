# Telugu Lesson — Day 28 (2026-07-05)

**Week 6 · Lesson 3/5 · 📅 Days, Time & Dates**

- **Telugu:** ఈరోజు / నిన్న / రేపు
- **Speak:** /Īrōju / Ninna / Rēpu/
- **Meaning:** Today / Yesterday / Tomorrow
- **Example:** ఈరోజు వేడిగా ఉంది — It's hot today
- **Tip:** These three are used constantly — master them first
