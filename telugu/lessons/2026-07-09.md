# Telugu Lesson — Day 32 (2026-07-09)

**Week 7 · Lesson 2/5 · 🍛 Food & Eating**

- **Telugu:** భోజనం చేశారా?
- **Speak:** /Bhōjanaṁ cēśārā?/
- **Meaning:** Have you eaten? (formal greeting!)
- **Example:** భోజనం చేశారా? — Have you had your meal?
- **Tip:** This IS a greeting in Telugu culture — deeply warm
