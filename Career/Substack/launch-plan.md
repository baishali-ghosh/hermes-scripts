# Substack Launch Plan

## Publication Concept

**Niche:** Engineering & agentic orchestration — from a woman's lens

**Target audience:** Women in tech

**Format:** Short punchy posts (400–600 words), one insight, one actionable takeaway

**Cadence:** Weekly (Wednesdays)

---

## Name Ideas

- *Wired Differently* — engineering & AI from a woman's lens
- *Agentic & Ambitious* — building in the AI orchestration era
- *The Connector* — double meaning (IS connectors + connecting ideas)
- *System Prompt* — short, punchy, techy

**Tagline:** *Short reads on engineering, agentic AI, and navigating tech as a woman who builds.*
